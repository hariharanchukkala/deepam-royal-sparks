## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-premium, mobile-first visual architecture for luxury ceremonial pyrotechnics. Rejecting the noisy, kitsch-laden tropes of standard retail firework packaging, the aesthetic draws inspiration from heritage Indian royal celebrations, royal court seals, and high-end atelier e-commerce.

The visual direction marries **Warm Heritage Elegance** with **Technical Precision**. It projects celebration, grandeur, and uncompromised safety (PESO compliance and certified green formulations) to high-net-worth patrons, royal weddings, grand festival planners, and luxury hospitality buyers. 

Key attributes:
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Creamy, ivory paper-like surfaces that evoke heirloom parchment and gold-leaf stationery.
- **Editorial Regality:** High-contrast vermilion and royal maroon focal points accented with luminous champagne gold details.
- **Technical Rigor:** Precise, clean tabular data layers displaying sound thresholds, aerial diameter, chemical smoke profile, and burn durations.
- **Conscious Festivity:** Prominent architectural badging system denoting eco-friendly certification, zero-barium compounds, and authorized institutional shipping.

## Colors

The palette is tuned around tactile, radiant festive papercraft and precious metals:

- **Primary (`#661023` - Kumkum Maroon):** A deep, rich ceremonial crimson anchor used for high-tier CTAs, signature typography, and prime structural accents.
- **Secondary (`#C59B27` - Champagne Gold):** A refined brushed-gold accent for badges, rating stars, premium spark tiers, and gold-foiled framing edges.
- **Tertiary (`#204632` - Royal Forest Green):** Reserved strictly for eco-safety metrics, PESO certification tags, CSIR-NEERI Green Cracker authenticity seals, and direct WhatsApp commerce buttons.
- **Neutral (`#241E1A` - Roasted Espresso):** Deep charcoal-espresso grounding all body copy and technical metrics, avoiding the harshness of pure `#000000` against warm backdrops.
- **Canvas Base:** Soft warm ivory (`#FDFBF7`) for the global canvas with warm cream card fills (`#F7F2EA`), evoking handmade archival stock.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear distinction between celebration and technical reassurance:

- **Editorial Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys regal Indian craftsmanship, festive legacy, and artisanal value. Display sizes utilize delicate negative tracking and generous line heights to preserve elegance.
- **Modern Utilitarian Mechanics (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for high legibility across dense mobile screens. It powers product naming extensions, fire safety warnings, decibel ratings, and step-by-step pyrotechnic ignition guides.
- **Labeling & Micro-specs:** Sub-labels, certified green cracker seals, and sound tier counters leverage uppercase `label-sm` with widened tracking (+0.06em) for clarity on compact screens.

## Layout & Spacing

A strictly regulated, fluid 4-column layout on mobile expands to an 8-column layout on tablet and a 12-column layout on desktop, capping at a maximum width of 1280px.

- **Mobile Viewport (< 640px):** Employs a fixed outer canvas margin of `1rem` (16px) with 16px gutters between adjacent cards. Multi-card carousels utilize bleed-to-edge scrolling with initial margin snap.
- **Tablet Viewport (640px - 1024px):** Increases outer canvas margins to `1.5rem` (24px) for 2-column or 3-column structured catalogue discovery.
- **Desktop Viewport (> 1024px):** Centralized 12-column layout centered with `3rem` margins to preserve high-end white space.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Micro-paddings inside metadata clusters use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px). Structural separation between specification pills and action trays strictly uses `space-md` (16px) to avoid visual noise.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through delicate tonal stratification and low-contrast golden hairline rules rather than heavy digital drop shadows:

- **Base Layer:** Natural matte Ivory surface (`#FDFBF7`).
- **Product Tiles & Sheet Surfaces:** Raised using a warm container tint (`#F7F2EA`) framed by an ultra-thin 1px border (`#EBDDCB`).
- **Hover & Active States:** Subtly elevated using an ambient, warm-tinted shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(102, 16, 35, 0.08)`.
- **Modals, Floating Trays, and WhatsApp Drawers:** Backed with frosted glass warmth (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) and anchored with a top hairline stroke of brushed champagne gold (`rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.4)`).
- **Physical Depth Cue:** Gold foil-embossed stamps utilize an inset hairline highlight: `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.

## Shapes

The design uses **Soft (Level 1)** geometric profiling. Radii are disciplined and deliberate (4px default, 8px on product cards, and 12px on overlay panels), evoking crisp die-cut ceremonial boxes and stamped royal envelopes rather than bubbly application interfaces. 

Interactive tags, technical badges, and experience chips feature crisp 4px corners. Strict square aspects are used on thumbnail grids, while primary action buttons use a subtle 4px corner curve to retain crispness and architectural weight.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Regal Button:** Deep vermilion/maroon (`#661023`) background, white text (`#FFFFFF`), 4px border-radius, minimum 48px height on mobile. Framed by a subtle internal gold edge on press.
- **Secondary Champagne Button:** Warm cream background with a crisp 1px border in brushed gold (`#C59B27`), text in `#661023`.
- **WhatsApp Concierge Button:** Deep Forest Green (`#204632`) or WhatsApp Teal (`#128C7E`) fill, crisp white text, accompanied by an authentic WhatsApp micro-icon, optimized for instant bulk order queries and institutional verification.

### Badges & Metadata Chips
- **PESO & Certified Green Cracker Badge:** High-contrast pill badge with an emerald hairline border, `#204632` text, displaying official QR-verification and CSIR-NEERI accreditation status.
- **Experience Spec Matrix:** A 3-tier compact horizontal widget displaying:
  1. *Sound Index:* Decibel metric (e.g., `85 dB • Low Acoustic`) with acoustic wave icon.
  2. *Aerial Canopy:* Flight height and spread (e.g., `120 ft • Golden Willow`).
  3. *Burn Duration:* Radial countdown icon (e.g., `45s Continuous`).

### Product Cards
- Warm container canvas (`#F7F2EA`) framed with 1px border (`#EBDDCB`).
- Square 1:1 media box supporting high-resolution daytime packaging renders alongside low-light night sky ignition videos.
- Fixed bottom drawer containing pricing in bold roasted espresso (`#241E1A`), green certificate indicator, and instant "Add to Hamper" quick-action.

### Inputs & Selectors
- Flat fields with `#FDFBF7` background, 1px `#D9CCA9` border, turning to 1.5px `#661023` on focus with no glowing ring. Text in `#241E1A`.
- Festive Quantity Stepper: Compact horizontal stepper with vermilion increment glyphs and ivory counter chamber.

### WhatsApp Commerce Drawer
- Persistent mobile bottom-dock for curated Diwali hampers, wedding consignments, and permit consultation, allowing single-tap export of the selected cart manifest directly into a verified enterprise WhatsApp thread.
